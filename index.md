# Privacy Policy — Λεξούλες

**Last updated:** 27 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

Λεξούλες (“the App”) is an educational application designed to help children learn to read Greek letters, syllables, and words.

Your privacy is important to us. The App is designed to work locally on your device and does not require an account or registration.

## 2. Information Collection and Use

The App does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It does not include advertising or analytics services.

Learning activities are performed on the device. The developer does not receive information about how the App is used.

## 3. Microphone and Audio

The App may request microphone access for its pronunciation-recording feature.

Audio is used locally on the device for the feature initiated by the user. The App does not transmit audio recordings to the developer.

Microphone access is only needed when the user chooses to use this feature. It can be denied in the device settings, although the recording feature will then be unavailable.

## 4. Suggestions and Sharing

The App includes an optional feature that allows users or parents to suggest corrections or improvements.

When this feature is used, the App opens the device’s system sharing interface. The user chooses whether to share the suggestion and which application or recipient to use.

The App does not automatically send suggestions to the developer. If the user chooses to send a suggestion using an external service, that service processes the information according to its own privacy policy.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally transmit personal information to third parties. No advertising or analytics SDKs are used.

The operating system and any external application voluntarily selected by the user may process information independently under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for children and their parents or caregivers. It does not require children to provide personal information, create accounts, or register.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

## 7. Data Security and Retention

The developer does not receive or maintain personal information through the App. Any information retained locally by the device is subject to the device's storage, backup, and security settings.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will be made available at the public URL where this policy is hosted.
